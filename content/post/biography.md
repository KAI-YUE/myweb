ML researcher turned AI product engineer and technical lead, working across agent workflows, product architecture, and engineering delivery. At Jobdream, I translate merchant operations into agent systems and oversee implementation quality. I’m also building an agentic game creation and publishing platform. Previously, I built an investment-education membership business reaching approximately 300–400 paying members and an estimated $9,000–$12,000 in peak monthly recurring revenue.

<!--more-->

## Work

- **Technical Lead — Agent Architecture & Engineering Delivery · 2025–Present.** At Jobdream, a private AI education and applied agent systems company founded in 2020, translate B2B merchant requirements into modular workflows; test product behavior, review architecture and code, and provide technical oversight for outsourced teams of one to several engineers.
- **Independent Builder — Agentic Game Platform.** Build a game creation and publishing platform that coordinates code, 2D animation, shaders, and other assets. Use Codex in a reviewable feature-delivery loop spanning scoped implementation, diff review, automated checks, and testing against live product behavior.
- **Founder — Investment Education Membership Business · 2023–2025.** Built a $30/month paid membership reaching approximately 300–400 paying members and an estimated $9,000–$12,000 in peak monthly recurring revenue. Grew the YouTube audience beyond 9,000 subscribers and converted viewers into paid Telegram members. Deliberately wound down active membership as AI tutoring commoditized the service’s core explanations.

## Writing

[Math May Be Solved. Coding May Be Solved. But Not Philosophy.](/writing/what-remains-valuable/) — reflections on product judgment as AI makes execution cheaper, and testing those judgments through Dotcom Déjà Vu.

## Working Together

I’m open to co-founder conversations around the game platform or joining an early-stage team building agentic products. I’m looking for teammates who care about product judgment, engineering delivery, commercial viability, and the effect their products have on people.
